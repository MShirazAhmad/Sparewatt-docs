# Dashboard and history

**⌥⌘R** opens the dashboard from anywhere. While it is open, Sparewatt shows in the Dock and in ⌘-Tab.

- **Monitor**: Overview, Processor, Graphics, Memory, Storage, Temperature, Network, Battery, Power flow, History.
- **Control**: Switches, On Battery, Extreme Saver, Watchlist, Shelf, Profiles & Alerts, Menu Bar, Startup Items, AI
  Assistant, This App, Privacy.

Monitor pages show the same cards as the menu bar panels, with more detail and 24-hour trends.

## Find a setting

The search field at the top of the sidebar finds any setting by its words: type "login" or "clipboard" and each match
is listed with where it is (*Menu Bar › General*); click it to open the page
([more](Dashboard-history-and-this-app.md#find-a-setting)).

## Overview and Mac health

The Overview starts with **what needs action** and **what is worth a look**, then a grid of every reading.

## Who is using it

Processor, graphics, memory and energy split into macOS, Apple Intelligence & Siri, indexing / sync / backup, your apps
and other tools. Sparewatt learns each background process's normal level, lists the **always-on** and **now-and-then**
ones, and highlights one using far more than usual.

## History

**Replay my day**, at the top of the page, plays a day back in 30 seconds from the saved readings; drag its strip to any moment.

Battery charge, power use, processor load and temperature over 1 hour to 30 days (one sample a minute, kept 30 days),
then memory used, swap, graphics, network and disk (kept since 1.20), and a log of **every stretch on battery**: how
long, how much charge it used, and which apps used the most power. **Energy used per app** shows today, 7 or 14 days
in watt-hours, split into time on battery and on the charger.

**Export CSV** saves all of it: the minute samples, the battery stretches and each day's energy per app.

## Share my day

**Share my day** (top of the Overview, or the icon next to *Export CSV* on History) makes a "My Mac today" picture: time
on battery and charge used today, today's charge curve, the app that used most battery, battery health and the charger.
Share it, copy it or save it as a PNG. It holds no file paths, names or serial numbers
([more](Dashboard-history-and-this-app.md#my-mac-today-share-card)). The *Day / Week*
switch makes "My Mac this week" instead: the last 7 days on battery, the top three battery apps, battery health and the
internet data used ([more](Dashboard-history-and-this-app.md#my-mac-this-week-card)).

## This App

What Sparewatt itself uses — processor time and memory — split by feature (Switches and the once-a-minute insights
included), with the settings that change it. Slower
update speed (Dashboard → Menu Bar → *Look*) costs less.

*Export settings…* and *Import settings…* move your settings, switches and profiles to another Mac, and *Show welcome
again* opens the first-run window
([more](Dashboard-history-and-this-app.md#your-settings-export-import-welcome-again)).

The page also has **Report a problem…**, and after a crash a card with **Report…**, **Show log** and **Dismiss**; see
[Troubleshooting](Troubleshooting.md#sparewatt-crashed-or-something-is-wrong).

Under *Trust*: *What Sparewatt runs and reads* opens the **Privacy** page, and *Uninstall Sparewatt…* removes the app
and what it keeps, with a list to untick first
([more](Dashboard-history-and-this-app.md#uninstall-sparewatt)).

## Privacy

Every program Sparewatt starts, every place it reads or writes, the permissions it may ask for and its network use, in
one list. The guide page [What Sparewatt runs and reads](What-Sparewatt-runs-and-reads.md) has the same
list.
