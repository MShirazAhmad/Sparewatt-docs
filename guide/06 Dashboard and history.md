# Dashboard and history

**⌥⌘R** opens the dashboard from anywhere. While it is open, Sparewatt shows in the Dock and in ⌘-Tab.

- **Monitor**: Overview, Processor, Graphics, Memory, Storage, Temperature, Network, Battery, Power flow, History.
- **Control**: Switches, On Battery, Extreme Saver, Watchlist, Shelf, Profiles & Alerts, Menu Bar, Startup Items, This
  App.

Monitor pages show the same cards as the menu bar panels, with more detail and 24-hour trends.

## Overview and Mac health

The Overview starts with **what needs action** and **what is worth a look**, then a grid of every reading.

## Who is using it

Processor, graphics, memory and energy split into macOS, Apple Intelligence & Siri, indexing / sync / backup, your apps
and other tools. Sparewatt learns each background process's normal level, lists the **always-on** and **now-and-then**
ones, and highlights one using far more than usual.

## History

Battery charge, power use, processor load and temperature over 1 hour to 30 days (one sample a minute, kept 30 days),
and a log of **every stretch on battery**: how long, how much charge it used, and which apps used the most power.

**Export CSV** saves both: the minute samples and the battery stretches.

## This App

What Sparewatt itself uses — processor time and memory — split by feature (Switches and the once-a-minute insights
included), with the settings that change it. Slower
update speed (Dashboard → Menu Bar → *Look*) costs less.
