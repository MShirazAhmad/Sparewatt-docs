# Features

Every Sparewatt feature: what it is for, what it does, when to use it, and what you gain. The [guide](../guide) explains
how to use the app step by step; these pages go feature by feature.

## What Sparewatt is for

A Mac shows you **that** the battery drains, the fan spins or the disk is full — not **why**, and not what to do. Sparewatt
answers three questions from the menu bar:

1. **What is my Mac doing right now?** Live processor, graphics, memory, storage, temperature, network, battery and power
   readings, in plain words.
2. **Who is doing it?** Every reading names the apps and background processes behind it, explains cryptic macOS parts,
   learns what is normal and flags what isn't.
3. **What can I do about it?** Quit what you don't use, freeze what you need later, change what the Mac may do on battery,
   switch local services off, clean up storage — all with confirmation and nothing deleted outright.

## Use cases: which features help when

**A full day on battery (lectures, conference, travel).** In the morning, check the [charge plan](05%20Battery.md#charge-plan). Pick the *Lecture* or *Travel* [profile](07%20Power%20controls.md#profiles). Let [idle-app suggestions](06%20Power.md#idle-apps-on-battery) close what you forgot. Set [Extreme Saver](07%20Power%20controls.md#extreme-saver) to turn on by itself at 20%. Afterwards, [History](11%20Dashboard%2C%20history%20and%20this%20app.md#history) shows what the day cost.

**"My battery drains fast and I don't know why."** Open [power per app](06%20Power.md#power-per-app-share-of-battery-drain) and the [time left](06%20Power.md#time-left). Check [background activity](04%20Who%20is%20using%20it.md#background-activity-and-what-is-normal) for anything unusual and [busy with no window](04%20Who%20is%20using%20it.md#busy-with-no-window-on-screen) for hidden apps. Then add a [Watchlist](04%20Who%20is%20using%20it.md#watchlist) rule for the offender.

**The battery drops overnight.** [While asleep](05%20Battery.md#while-asleep-last-24-hours) names the wake reasons. Turn off Power Nap and network wakes in [On Battery settings](07%20Power%20controls.md#on-battery-settings).

**Running local AI models or dev servers.** Put Ollama, LM Studio, databases and web servers on [Switches](10%20Switches.md) with *Stop on battery*. Watch the [graphics panel](02%20Processor%2C%20graphics%20and%20memory.md#graphics-panel) and [memory pressure](02%20Processor%2C%20graphics%20and%20memory.md#memory-panel) while a model runs.

**The Mac is hot or loud.** The [temperature panel](03%20Temperature%2C%20network%20and%20storage%20space.md#temperature-and-fans) shows which part is hot and the fan speed. The processor and graphics lists name the app, and [Quit / Force Quit](04%20Who%20is%20using%20it.md#look-closer-then-quit-or-force-quit) stops a runaway process.

**The disk is almost full.** See [where the space went](03%20Temperature%2C%20network%20and%20storage%20space.md#storage-space), including purgeable space and System Data. Then use [Downloads tidy-up](09%20Files%20and%20storage.md#downloads-tidy-up) and [app caches](09%20Files%20and%20storage.md#app-caches). The *Storage almost full* notification warns before it gets critical.

**Writing and sending files all day.** Use [Latest File](09%20Files%20and%20storage.md#latest-file) to drag what you just exported, the [Shelf](09%20Files%20and%20storage.md#the-shelf) to collect attachments from several folders, and the [clipboard](09%20Files%20and%20storage.md#clipboard) to paste references as plain text.

**Charging problems.** The [charger check](06%20Power.md#charger-check) catches a charger that's too small. ["Battery state doesn't add up"](05%20Battery.md#battery-state-doesnt-add-up) catches a charge limiter that isn't holding. [Power flow](06%20Power.md#power-flow) shows how fast it really charges.

**Asking an AI assistant instead of reading panels.** Turn on the [MCP server](12%20AI%20Assistant.md) and ask Claude Code, Codex or another assistant "what is draining my battery?" or "which app used the most memory today?". It reads the same lists and history you see. Turn on *Allow changes* only if you want it to switch services or quit apps for you.

**A new or second-hand Mac.** Look at [battery health and wear](05%20Battery.md#health-card), [Startup Items](11%20Dashboard%2C%20history%20and%20this%20app.md#startup-items) for what installs left behind, and [Mac health](11%20Dashboard%2C%20history%20and%20this%20app.md#overview-and-mac-health) for anything that needs action.
